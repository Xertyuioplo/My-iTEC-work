Top 5 minecraft mobs

number 5: zombie

the zombie is a underrated mob butit is very useful. it provides a food source when killed and playing superflat uses this mob alot. Providing food and iron and carrots is what makes this mob very good.

Number 4: creeper

The creeper is usually not useful, exept if you dont have a pickaxe. If your pickaxe breaks and you find an ore, you can lead a creeper there and explode it, dropping the ore! Just dont blow yourself up. Also great for fireworks.

Number 3: skeleton

The skeleton is good because it drops bones. The bones can then be turned into bonemeal which is useful for farms. They can also have armor on them, dropping it when they are killed.

Number 2: Wandering trader

The wandering trader is also useful for superflat, because you can buy saplings and seeds from him. He also has llamas which drop leather.

Number 1: Villager

The villager is important for trading and getting emeralds. The toolsmiths and weaponsmiths can trade diamond armor and tools for emeralds. They also hve good prices when you defeat a raid.
